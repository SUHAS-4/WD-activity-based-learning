With over 22 official languages, thousands of culinary delicacies, and monuments spanning millennia, every corner of India tells a story waiting to be experienced firsthand by travelers worldwide.

Best Seasons to Visit

October to March offers pleasant weather across most of North and South India. Monsoon season (June-September) is exquisite for Kerala and Meghalaya.

Cultural Etiquette

Remove footwear before entering temples or homes. Dress modestly at religious sanctuaries and ask permission before photographing local artisans.

Local Transit & Rules

Indian Railways (IRCTC) offers vast nationwide connectivity. Use verified ride-hailing apps or prepaid taxis at airports for hassle-free transit.

Helpful Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries regarding monument tickets, ideal seasons, and travel guidelines.

The ideal general tourist season runs from October to March when temperatures are mild and pleasant across Delhi, Rajasthan, Agra, and South India. Himalayan regions like Himachal Pradesh and Ladakh are best visited from May to September.
The "Golden Triangle" circuit (Delhi, Agra, and Jaipur) is the most popular choice for first-time visitors, offering iconic monuments like the Taj Mahal alongside vibrant bazaars and palaces. Kerala is also ideal for a serene, tropical introduction.
Most ASI (Archaeological Survey of India) national monuments are open from sunrise to sunset. However, specific monuments like the Taj Mahal remain closed on Fridays. It is always recommended to pre-book ticket passes online via official ASI portals.
Respect religious sanctuaries by removing shoes before entry. Covering shoulders and knees is appropriate in temples and mosques. Greeting with a traditional "Namaste" (palms pressed together) is universally appreciated.
